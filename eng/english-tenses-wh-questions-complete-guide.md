## 1️⃣ Present Tense + WH Questions

### 🔹 Present Simple (বর্তমান সাধারণ)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + V1/V1+s/es + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + do/does + not + V1 + Object  
- **Question**: Do/Does + Subject + V1 + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I eat rice. (আমি ভাত খাই)
✅ He eats rice. (সে ভাত খায়)
❌ She do not eat rice. (সে ভাত খায় না)
✅ She does not eat rice. (সে ভাত খায় না)
❌ Do he eat rice? (সে কি ভাত খায়?)
✅ Does he eat rice? (সে কি ভাত খায়?)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```
I wake up at 6 AM. (আমি সকাল ৬টায় ঘুম থেকে উঠি)
She works in a bank. (সে একটি ব্যাংকে কাজ করে)
We play football every Friday. (আমরা প্রতি শুক্রবার ফুটবল খেলি)
The sun rises in the east. (সূর্য পূর্ব দিকে উদয় হয়)
Water boils at 100°C. (পানি ১০০ ডিগ্রি সেলসিয়াসে ফুটে)
```

#### 50 Practice Questions:
1. I _____ (go) to school every day. **Answer: go**
2. She _____ (not like) coffee. **Answer: does not like**
3. _____ you _____ (speak) English? **Answer: Do, speak**
4. He _____ (watch) TV in the evening. **Answer: watches**
5. We _____ (not have) a car. **Answer: do not have**
6. _____ they _____ (live) in Dhaka? **Answer: Do, live**
7. My mother _____ (cook) delicious food. **Answer: cooks**
8. The train _____ (arrive) at 8 PM. **Answer: arrives**
9. Children _____ (love) chocolate. **Answer: love**
10. _____ she _____ (work) on Sundays? **Answer: Does, work**

### 🔹 Present Continuous (বর্তমান চলমান)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + am/is/are + V1+ing + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + am/is/are + not + V1+ing + Object
- **Question**: Am/Is/Are + Subject + V1+ing + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I am eating rice. (আমি ভাত খাচ্ছি)
✅ He is eating rice. (সে ভাত খাচ্ছে)
✅ They are eating rice. (তারা ভাত খাচ্ছে)
❌ I am eat rice. (ভুল)
❌ He eating rice. (ভুল)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```
I am reading a book now. (আমি এখন একটি বই পড়ছি)
She is cooking dinner. (সে রাতের খাবার রান্না করছে)
We are watching a movie. (আমরা একটি সিনেমা দেখছি)
It is raining outside. (বাইরে বৃষ্টি হচ্ছে)
The children are playing in the garden. (বাচ্চারা বাগানে খেলছে)
```

#### 50 Practice Questions:
1. I _____ (write) a letter now. **Answer: am writing**
2. She _____ (not sleep) at the moment. **Answer: is not sleeping**
3. _____ you _____ (listen) to music? **Answer: Are, listening**
4. He _____ (run) in the park. **Answer: is running**
5. We _____ (not watch) TV right now. **Answer: are not watching**
6. _____ they _____ (come) to the party? **Answer: Are, coming**
7. My father _____ (drive) to work. **Answer: is driving**
8. The baby _____ (cry) loudly. **Answer: is crying**
9. Students _____ (study) for exams. **Answer: are studying**
10. _____ it _____ (rain) heavily? **Answer: Is, raining**

### 🔹 Present Perfect (বর্তমান পূর্ণ)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + have/has + V3 + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + have/has + not + V3 + Object
- **Question**: Have/Has + Subject + V3 + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I have eaten rice. (আমি ভাত খেয়েছি)
✅ He has eaten rice. (সে ভাত খেয়েছে)
❌ I have ate rice. (ভুল)
❌ He have eaten rice. (ভুল)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```
I have finished my homework. (আমি আমার বাড়ির কাজ শেষ করেছি)
She has visited London twice. (সে দুইবার লন্ডন গেছে)
We have lived here for 5 years. (আমরা এখানে ৫ বছর ধরে থাকছি)
They have just arrived. (তারা এইমাত্র পৌঁছেছে)
Have you ever been to Cox's Bazar? (তুমি কি কখনো কক্সবাজার গেছো?)
```

### 🔹 Present Perfect Continuous (বর্তমান পূর্ণ চলমান)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + have/has + been + V1+ing + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + have/has + not + been + V1+ing + Object
- **Question**: Have/Has + Subject + been + V1+ing + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I have been eating rice for 10 minutes. (আমি ১০ মিনিট ধরে ভাত খাচ্ছি)
✅ He has been eating rice since morning. (সে সকাল থেকে ভাত খাচ্ছে)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```
I have been studying English for 2 years. (আমি ২ বছর ধরে ইংরেজি পড়ছি)
She has been working here since 2020. (সে ২০২০ সাল থেকে এখানে কাজ করছে)
We have been waiting for the bus for 30 minutes. (আমরা ৩০ মিনিট ধরে বাসের জন্য অপেক্ষা করছি)
It has been raining since yesterday. (গতকাল থেকে বৃষ্টি হচ্ছে)
They have been living in this house for 10 years. (তারা ১০ বছর ধরে এই বাড়িতে থাকছে)
```

### 🔹 Present Tense WH Questions

#### What Questions (Present):
```
What do you do? (তুমি কি কাজ করো?) → I am a teacher.
What are you doing? (তুমি কি করছো?) → I am reading.
What have you done? (তুমি কি করেছো?) → I have finished my work.
What have you been doing? (তুমি কি করে আসছো?) → I have been studying.
```

#### Where Questions (Present):
```
Where do you live? (তুমি কোথায় থাকো?) → I live in Dhaka.
Where are you going? (তুমি কোথায় যাচ্ছো?) → I am going to school.
Where have you been? (তুমি কোথায় গিয়েছিলে?) → I have been to the market.
Where have you been working? (তুমি কোথায় কাজ করে আসছো?) → I have been working at home.
```

#### When Questions (Present):
```
When do you wake up? (তুমি কখন ঘুম থেকে ওঠো?) → I wake up at 6 AM.
When are you coming? (তুমি কখন আসছো?) → I am coming now.
When have you finished? (তুমি কখন শেষ করেছো?) → I have just finished.
When have you been studying? (তুমি কখন থেকে পড়ে আসছো?) → I have been studying since morning.
```

#### Who Questions (Present):
```
Who teaches you? (তোমাকে কে পড়ায়?) → Mr. Rahman teaches me.
Who is calling? (কে ফোন করছে?) → My mother is calling.
Who has helped you? (তোমাকে কে সাহায্য করেছে?) → My friend has helped me.
Who has been helping you? (তোমাকে কে সাহায্য করে আসছে?) → My brother has been helping me.
```

#### Why Questions (Present):
```
Why do you study English? (তুমি কেন ইংরেজি পড়ো?) → Because it's important.
Why are you crying? (তুমি কেন কাঁদছো?) → Because I am sad.
Why have you come here? (তুমি এখানে কেন এসেছো?) → I have come to meet you.
Why have you been waiting? (তুমি কেন অপেক্ষা করে আসছো?) → I have been waiting for the bus.
```

#### How Questions (Present):
```
How do you go to work? (তুমি কিভাবে কাজে যাও?) → I go by bus.
How are you feeling? (তুমি কেমন অনুভব করছো?) → I am feeling good.
How have you learned this? (তুমি এটা কিভাবে শিখেছো?) → I have learned from books.
How have you been managing? (তুমি কিভাবে সামলে আসছো?) → I have been managing well.
```

## 2️⃣ Past Tense + WH Questions

### 🔹 Past Simple (অতীত সাধারণ)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + V2 + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + did + not + V1 + Object
- **Question**: Did + Subject + V1 + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I ate rice yesterday. (আমি গতকাল ভাত খেয়েছিলাম)
✅ He went to school. (সে স্কুলে গিয়েছিল)
❌ I did ate rice. (ভুল)
❌ Did he went to school? (ভুল)
✅ Did he go to school? (সে কি স্কুলে গিয়েছিল?)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```
I woke up at 7 AM yesterday. (আমি গতকাল সকাল ৭টায় ঘুম থেকে উঠেছিলাম)
She finished her work last night. (সে গত রাতে তার কাজ শেষ করেছিল)
We visited our grandparents last week. (আমরা গত সপ্তাহে আমাদের দাদা-দাদীর সাথে দেখা করেছিলাম)
The movie started at 8 PM. (সিনেমা রাত ৮টায় শুরু হয়েছিল)
They played cricket in the afternoon. (তারা বিকেলে ক্রিকেট খেলেছিল)
```

#### 50 Practice Questions:
1. I _____ (go) to the market yesterday. **Answer: went**
2. She _____ (not come) to the party last night. **Answer: did not come**
3. _____ you _____ (see) the movie? **Answer: Did, see**
4. He _____ (buy) a new phone last week. **Answer: bought**
5. We _____ (not have) dinner at home. **Answer: did not have**
6. _____ they _____ (visit) the museum? **Answer: Did, visit**
7. My mother _____ (cook) biryani yesterday. **Answer: cooked**
8. The train _____ (leave) on time. **Answer: left**
9. Children _____ (enjoy) the picnic. **Answer: enjoyed**
10. _____ she _____ (call) you last night? **Answer: Did, call**

### 🔹 Past Continuous (অতীত চলমান)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + was/were + V1+ing + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + was/were + not + V1+ing + Object
- **Question**: Was/Were + Subject + V1+ing + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I was eating rice when he came. (সে আসার সময় আমি ভাত খাচ্ছিলাম)
✅ They were playing football at 5 PM. (বিকাল ৫টায় তারা ফুটবল খেলছিল)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```
I was reading a book when the phone rang. (ফোন বাজার সময় আমি একটি বই পড়ছিলাম)
She was cooking dinner at 7 PM. (সন্ধ্যা ৭টায় সে রাতের খাবার রান্না করছিল)
We were watching TV when the power went out. (বিদ্যুৎ চলে যাওয়ার সময় আমরা টিভি দেখছিলাম)
It was raining heavily last night. (গত রাতে প্রচণ্ড বৃষ্টি হচ্ছিল)
The children were sleeping when I arrived. (আমি পৌঁছানোর সময় বাচ্চারা ঘুমাচ্ছিল)
```

### 🔹 Past Perfect (অতীত পূর্ণ)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + had + V3 + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + had + not + V3 + Object
- **Question**: Had + Subject + V3 + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I had eaten rice before he came. (সে আসার আগে আমি ভাত খেয়ে ফেলেছিলাম)
✅ She had finished her work before the deadline. (সময়সীমার আগেই সে তার কাজ শেষ করে ফেলেছিল)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```

She had never seen snow before visiting Canada. (কানাডায় যাওয়ার আগে সে কখনো বরফ দেখেনি)
```

## 3️⃣ Future Tense + WH Questions

### 🔹 Future Simple (ভবিষ্যৎ সাধারণ)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + will + V1 + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + will + not + V1 + Object
- **Question**: Will + Subject + V1 + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I will eat rice tomorrow. (আমি কাল ভাত খাবো)
✅ She will not come to the party. (সে পার্টিতে আসবে না)
✅ Will you help me? (তুমি কি আমাকে সাহায্য করবে?)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:
```
I will call you tonight. (আমি আজ রাতে তোমাকে ফোন করবো)
She will start her new job next week. (সে আগামী সপ্তাহে তার নতুন কাজ শুরু করবে)
We will visit our relatives during Eid. (ঈদের সময় আমরা আমাদের আত্মীয়দের সাথে দেখা করবো)
The weather will be sunny tomorrow. (কাল আবহাওয়া রৌদ্রোজ্জ্বল থাকবে)
They will graduate next year. (তারা আগামী বছর স্নাতক হবে)
```

### 🔹 Future Continuous (ভবিষ্যৎ চলমান)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + will + be + V1+ing + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + will + not + be + V1+ing + Object
- **Question**: Will + Subject + be + V1+ing + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I will be eating rice at 8 PM. (রাত ৮টায় আমি ভাত খেতে থাকবো)
✅ She will be working tomorrow morning. (কাল সকালে সে কাজ করতে থাকবে)
```

#### Daily Use Examples:

### 🔹 Future Perfect (ভবিষ্যৎ পূর্ণ)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + will + have + V3 + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + will + not + have + V3 + Object
- **Question**: Will + Subject + have + V3 + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I will have finished my work by 6 PM. (সন্ধ্যা ৬টার মধ্যে আমি আমার কাজ শেষ করে ফেলবো)
✅ She will have graduated by next year. (আগামী বছরের মধ্যে সে স্নাতক হয়ে যাবে)
```

### 🔹 Future Perfect Continuous (ভবিষ্যৎ পূর্ণ চলমান)

#### Structure:
- **Positive**: Subject + will + have + been + V1+ing + Object
- **Negative**: Subject + will + not + have + been + V1+ing + Object
- **Question**: Will + Subject + have + been + V1+ing + Object?

#### Examples:
```
✅ I will have been working here for 5 years by December. (ডিসেম্বরের মধ্যে আমি এখানে ৫ বছর ধরে কাজ করতে থাকবো)
✅ She will have been studying for 3 hours by then. (তখন পর্যন্ত সে ৩ ঘন্টা ধরে পড়তে থাকবে)
```

## 6️⃣ Common Mistakes

### 🚫 Tense Mistakes

#### Present Tense Errors:
```
❌ I am go to school. 
✅ I go to school. (Simple) / I am going to school. (Continuous)

❌ She don't like tea.
✅ She doesn't like tea.

❌ He working in a bank.
✅ He works in a bank. / He is working in a bank.

❌ I have ate breakfast.
✅ I have eaten breakfast.
```

#### Past Tense Errors:
```
❌ I go to school yesterday.
✅ I went to school yesterday.

❌ She didn't came to the party.
✅ She didn't come to the party.

❌ I was went home.
✅ I went home. / I was going home.

❌ He has went to London.
✅ He has gone to London. / He went to London.
```

#### Future Tense Errors:
```
❌ I will went tomorrow.
✅ I will go tomorrow.

❌ She going to call you.
✅ She is going to call you. / She will call you.

❌ We will be go there.
✅ We will go there. / We will be going there.
```

### 🚫 WH Question Mistakes

#### Word Order Errors:
```
❌ What you are doing?
✅ What are you doing?

❌ Where you live?
✅ Where do you live?

❌ When you will come?
✅ When will you come?

❌ Why you are late?
✅ Why are you late?

❌ How you go to work?
✅ How do you go to work?
```

#### Auxiliary Verb Errors:
```
❌ What you eat for breakfast?
✅ What do you eat for breakfast?

❌ Where she works?
✅ Where does she work?

❌ When he will arrive?
✅ When will he arrive?

❌ Who you met yesterday?
✅ Who did you meet yesterday?
```

## 🎯 Quick Reference

### Tense Chart:
| Tense | Structure | Example | Bengali |
|-------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Present Simple | S + V1/V1+s/es | I eat rice | আমি ভাত খাই |
| Present Continuous | S + am/is/are + V1+ing | I am eating rice | আমি ভাত খাচ্ছি |
| Present Perfect | S + have/has + V3 | I have eaten rice | আমি ভাত খেয়েছি |
| Past Simple | S + V2 | I ate rice | আমি ভাত খেয়েছিলাম |
| Past Continuous | S + was/were + V1+ing | I was eating rice | আমি ভাত খাচ্ছিলাম |
| Future Simple | S + will + V1 | I will eat rice | আমি ভাত খাবো |

|---------|---------|---------|

| Who | কে | Who is calling? |
| Why | কেন | Why are you late? |

## 🏆 Final Tips

### Memory Techniques:
1. **Practice daily** - রোজ ১০টি করে বাক্য বলুন
2. **Use in conversation** - বন্ধুদের সাথে ইংরেজিতে কথা বলুন
3. **Write sentences** - প্রতিদিন ৫টি করে বাক্য লিখুন
4. **Listen to English** - ইংরেজি গান, সিনেমা দেখুন
5. **Think in English** - মনে মনে ইংরেজিতে ভাবুন

### Common Patterns:
- **Present**: do/does, am/is/are
- **Past**: did, was/were  
- **Future**: will, going to
